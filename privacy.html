1. What this policy covers

TYMRAK is a time-tracking service for employers and their employees. It includes the website at tymrak.com, the web portal at app.tymrak.com and the TYMRAK mobile app. This policy explains what personal information we handle, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

Who is in charge of the information. Your employer (the company that invited you to TYMRAK) decides what employee information is entered and how it is used for payroll, scheduling and time off. TYMRAK provides the service and handles the information on the employer's behalf. For questions about your hours, time off or employment, contact your employer. For questions about how TYMRAK handles information, contact us using the details above.

3. Location

When you tap Clock in or Clock out, TYMRAK asks your device for your precise location once, so your employer can see that the entry was made at the workplace. Your device asks for your permission first. If you decline, you cannot clock at a workplace that requires a location check. We store the coordinates with that time entry. We do not use location for advertising or for any purpose other than verifying clock entries.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the service: sign-in, clocking, timecards, approvals, time off and balances.
  • To let employers and managers run payroll preparation, scheduling and time off.
  • If your employer's admins enter hourly rates, to show them an estimate of gross pay for each pay period.
  • To send account emails, such as invitations, activation links and password resets.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not show advertising. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools in the app.

5. Who can see your information

  • Your employer's managers and administrators, within the employees and workplaces assigned to them. They can see your time records, clock locations, time-off requests and balances, and employment details. Only administrators see hourly pay rates and estimated pay; managers do not.
  • You, for your own records.
  • Your employer's accounting software, only if your employer connects it (section 7). Your approved hours are then sent to your employer's own QuickBooks Online company.
  • TYMRAK staff and contractors, only as needed to run, secure and support the service, under confidentiality duties.
  • Other employers cannot see your information. Each company's data is kept separate.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect rights and safety.

7. Accounting software (QuickBooks Online)

If your employer chooses to connect QuickBooks Online, an administrator of your employer signs in to Intuit and gives TYMRAK permission to work with that one QuickBooks company. Then:

  • What TYMRAK reads from QuickBooks: the company name and the list of active employees (name and email), only so your employer can match each TYMRAK employee to the right QuickBooks employee.
  • What TYMRAK sends to QuickBooks: after a manager approves a timecard, one entry per day with the date and your regular hours. Overtime and double time are shown to your employer to enter in payroll themselves. Nothing is sent before approval.
  • What TYMRAK never touches in QuickBooks: bank details, pay rates, paycheques, invoices, customers and any other QuickBooks records.
  • Security: the sign-in tokens Intuit gives us are stored encrypted and are only used by our servers. Your employer can disconnect at any time from TYMRAK or from QuickBooks; we then stop all access and revoke the tokens.
  • Once your hours are in QuickBooks, your employer and Intuit's own terms and privacy policy govern them there.

8. How long we keep information

TYMRAK does not delete records automatically. Employers decide how long records are kept, and employment-records laws may require them to keep hours and time-off records for a period. Records are kept until the employer asks us to remove them or until the account is deleted as described in section 9.

9. Deleting your account

You can ask for your account to be deleted from Delete my account in the employee pages. A manager processes the request, and we aim to do so within 30 days. When it is processed, your name becomes "Former Employee", your email and username are replaced with placeholders, your mobile number is cleared and your sign-in is removed. Your hours stay linked to the placeholder so your employer's payroll and audit records remain complete. Your position, department, province and hire date are kept because they are needed to apply overtime and vacation rules. Managers and administrators are removed by another administrator.

10. Your choices and rights

You can ask us, through your employer or directly, to:

  • see the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong (your employer can correct time records);
  • delete your account (section 9);
  • withdraw location permission in your device settings (you will not be able to clock at a workplace that requires a location check).

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under federal or provincial privacy law, including the right to complain to a privacy regulator, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

11. Security

Information travels over encrypted connections. Access is limited by role, and each company's data is kept separate in the database. We apply updates and review access. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell affected people and the employer promptly if a breach puts personal information at risk of significant harm, as the law requires.

12. Children

TYMRAK is for working people and the employers who manage them. It is not directed at children. Workers under 18 should use it only as their employer directs, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone who is not an employee or manager of an employer using TYMRAK.

13. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we will update the effective date and tell account holders, for example by email or inside the app.
