Common questions

Get to know Tymrak

Can employees use Tymrak on a phone?

Yes. Tymrak includes a mobile-friendly employee experience for recording work hours.

Can managers review timecards?

Yes. Managers can review timecards and approve recorded hours through the manager workflow.

Does Tymrak process payroll?

Tymrak is a time-tracking application designed to help prepare organized hours for payroll. It is not a payroll processing service.
